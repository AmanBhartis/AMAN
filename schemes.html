Government Schemes for Farmers

Discover key agriculture support programs, eligibility guidance, and how to apply.

Why these schemes matter

Krishi Sathi helps you find schemes that support crop insurance, loans, irrigation, soil health, and farmer income. These programs are designed to improve productivity, reduce risk, and increase access to farming resources.

PM Kisan Samman Nidhi

Provides direct income support to farmers with up to ₹6,000 per year in three equal installments.

  • Eligibility: Small and marginal landholder farmers.
  • Benefit: Income support for crop expenditure.
  • Apply: Through your local agriculture office or on PM Kisan portal.

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana

This crop insurance scheme protects farmers from losses due to natural disasters, pests, and crop failure.

  • Coverage: Kharif, Rabi, and commercial crops.
  • Premium: Subsidized by central and state governments.
  • Apply: Through your bank, insurance partner, or local nodal office.

Soil Health Card Scheme

Get a detailed soil health report for your land and receive fertilizer recommendations for better crop yields.

  • Benefit: Helps optimize nutrient use and reduce input cost.
  • Process: Soil sample collection and lab analysis.
  • Location: Visit the nearest soil testing center.

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Provides short-term credit for crop production and allied activities at low interest rates.

  • Loan Limit: Based on cropping pattern and scale.
  • Use: Seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, and farm equipment.
  • Apply: At your bank branch or through the KCC portal.

How to apply

  1. Visit the nearest agriculture or bank branch for scheme details.
  2. Collect required documents: land records, Aadhaar, bank account, and farmer ID.
  3. Submit the application either in person or through the official scheme portal.
  4. Follow up with local officers to track approval and payments.
